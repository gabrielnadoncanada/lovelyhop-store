A sensory table is only as good as how often it changes. The good news: changing the filler or the tools is often enough to make the same table feel new. Here are twenty ideas, from the simplest to the most elaborate.

<Callout title="Safety">
The ages given are guidelines. Before age 3, nothing small enough to swallow. Water and fine fillers always need an adult nearby. No water beads, at any age.
</Callout>

## The basic fillers

| Filler | From | Tools that work well |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm water | 1 year | Cups, funnels, sponges |
| Dry oats | 1 year | Big spoon, bowls |
| Moulding sand | 2 years | Moulds, shovel, sieve |
| Uncooked rice | 2½ years | Funnels, spoons, containers |
| Dry pasta | 3 years | Tongs, sorting trays |
| Snow | 2 years | Moulds, small shovels, warm water |

## Ages 1 to 2: discover

1. **Water and floating ducks**: push, sink, fish them out.
2. **Oats and spoons**: fill, pour, start again.
3. **Cold cooked pasta**: slippery, soft, easy to grab.
4. **Autumn leaves**: crumple, listen, sort big from small.
5. **Ice cubes**: cold, slippery, melting in the hand.

## Ages 2 to 4: pour and sort

6. **Rice and funnels**: flow from one bin to the other.
7. **Sand and a sieve**: separate the sand from hidden treasures.
8. **Washing doll dishes**: soapy water, a brush, a drying rack.
9. **Snow and food colouring**: drops of colour to paint the snow.
10. **Coloured bottle caps**: sort by colour, stack, count.
11. **Figurine bath**: water, bubbles, animals to wash.
12. **Soil and pots**: plant seeds in small pots.

## Ages 4 to 6: build and invent

13. **Marble chutes**: cut pieces to roll things from one bin to the other.
14. **Dinosaur dig**: "bones" buried in sand, a brush to uncover them.
15. **Pasta threading**: tube pasta and a lace.
16. **Colour mixing**: three glasses of coloured water, a dropper, empty containers.
17. **Block city**: lids closed, the table becomes a building board.
18. **Standing painting**: a smooth art tray, gouache and big brushes.
19. **Magnetic fishing**: cardboard fish with paper clips and a magnetic rod.
20. **Light shapes**: translucent shapes on a lit surface to watch colours blend.

## Revive a filler without emptying everything

Before changing everything, try changing **just one thing**: a new tool, a challenge ("fill this jar using only the spoon"), or a few hidden objects. The same filler becomes a new activity.
