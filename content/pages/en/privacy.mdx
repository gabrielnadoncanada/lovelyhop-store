## Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses two **essential** cookies, always on:

- `lh_cart`: keeps your cart for 30 days;
- `lh_consent`: remembers your cookie choices for six months.

The checkout page, hosted by Shopify, uses its own cookies needed for payment and fraud prevention.

**Analytics** and **advertising** cookies are off by default. They are only turned on if you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can withdraw your consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. [As of the date of this policy, no analytics or advertising tool is installed.]

## Your rights

At any time, you can:

- ask to access the personal information we hold about you;
- have it corrected if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
- withdraw your consent, for example to the newsletter or non-essential cookies;
- ask for your computerized information in a structured, commonly used technological format;
- ask for de-indexing or that dissemination of information stop, where the law allows.

Write to [email]. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the [Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/english/).

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS, payments are handled by a certified platform, and access to information is limited to the people who need it for their work. In the event of a confidentiality incident presenting a risk of serious injury, we will notify the people concerned and the Commission d'accès à l'information.

## Children

The site is intended for adults buying for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
